_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

This privacy policy covers both Peepochat and Peepochat Pocket. The short version is that I don't collect, store, or share any of your information.

## Definitions.

To keep things clear, these words have the following meanings throughout this policy.

- **"Peepochat"** means the web-based Twitch chat client available at [chat.rcw.lol](https://chat.rcw.lol), which runs in your web browser.
- **"Peepochat Pocket"** means the separate mobile app for Android and other mobile devices. It's a different app from Peepochat, but it works the same way when it comes to your privacy.
- **"The apps"** means Peepochat and Peepochat Pocket together. Anything in this policy that mentions "the apps" applies to both of them equally.
- **"I", "me", and "my"** mean Riley Wilson, the developer of the apps.
- **"You" and "your"** mean you, the person using either of the apps.
- **"Your device"** means whatever you use to run the apps, like your computer, phone, or tablet, along with the web browser you use Peepochat in.
- **"Personal information"** means any information that could be used to identify you, such as your name, email address, IP address, Twitch account, or chat messages.
- **"Third-party services"** means services the apps connect to that are run by someone other than me, like Twitch. These are listed in the [third-party services](#third-party-services) section below.

## What I collect.

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers, or ads in either app, and I don't run a server that receives your messages, settings, or Twitch account details. The only exception is the basic network information handled by my hosting provider, which is explained in the [hosting](#hosting) section below.

## What's stored on your device.

To work, the apps need to remember a few things. All of it is stored locally and never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself.

- **Your Twitch login** - the access token given to the app when you sign in with Twitch. Peepochat keeps it in your browser's local storage, and Peepochat Pocket keeps it in your device's secure storage.
- **Your settings** - channels, splits, layouts, highlights, custom sounds, and other preferences.
- **Cached data** - things like emote and badge lists, so they don't have to be downloaded every time.

You can remove all of this at any time by logging out, clearing your data from the settings, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app.

## Hosting.

Peepochat is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com), and your use of it is also subject to [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare's web analytics are turned off for Peepochat.

Like any website host, Cloudflare has to process some information about each visit in order to deliver the site and protect it from attacks and abuse. According to their privacy policy, this can include your IP address and other information about the traffic between your device and the site. Cloudflare handles this information on my behalf, and I don't use it to identify or track you.

Cloudflare may also set a small number of strictly necessary cookies, which are used for security features like bot protection. These aren't used for advertising or tracking. You can read more in [Cloudflare's cookie policy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

## Third-party services.

Like any Twitch chat client, the apps connect directly from your device to a few third-party services in order to work. Those requests go straight to each service, not through me, and each one has its own privacy policy.

- **Twitch** - for signing in, sending and receiving chat messages, and channel information. ([Privacy policy](https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/))
- **BetterTTV**, **FrankerFaceZ**, and **7TV** - for third-party emotes and badges.
- **IVR** ([api.ivr.fi](https://api.ivr.fi/v2/docs)) - for extra public user and emote information.
- **Recent Messages** ([recent-messages.robotty.de](https://recent-messages.robotty.de)) - for loading chat history when you join a channel.
- **rcw.lol** - for downloading the list of OwO+ member badges. Nothing about you is sent with this request.
- **Google Fonts** - only in Peepochat, and only if you choose a custom chat font.

## Children's privacy.

Since nothing is collected, no information from anyone, including children under 13, is collected either. You still need to follow Twitch's own age requirements to use your Twitch account.
